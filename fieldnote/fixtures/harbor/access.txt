Harbor Workshop - access policy, effective 2026-09-01. Harbor Workshop is a fictional teaching fixture.

Members may enter the workshop from 08:00 to 20:00 on weekdays. Weekend entry requires a booking recorded by the coordinator.

Visitors must stay with a member. A visitor cannot use the cutting room, even when accompanied.

Lost access cards must be reported to the coordinator. The replacement fee is 12 credits. This policy does not specify how quickly a replacement is issued.
